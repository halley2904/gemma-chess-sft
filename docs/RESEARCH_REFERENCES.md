# Research references

1. Ruoss et al. (2024), *Amortized Planning with Large-Scale Transformers: A Case Study on Chess* (Searchless Chess / ChessBench).
   - Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04494
   - Code/data instructions: https://github.com/google-deepmind/searchless_chess
   - Use as a research reference and potential source of engine-labelled data. Respect the mixed licences attached to individual assets.

2. Lichess Open Database.
   - https://database.lichess.org/
   - Database tooling: https://github.com/lichess-org/database
   - Lichess describes its exports as CC0; record exact export and retrieval date.

3. Gemma 3 270M IT.
   - https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-270m-it
   - Accept Google's Gemma terms before download and follow the model card.

4. python-chess.
   - https://python-chess.readthedocs.io/
   - Used for PGN parsing, FEN checks, legal moves and Stockfish communication.
